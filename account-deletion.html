MARU GHAR — Account deletion request

You can delete your MARU GHAR customer account from inside the app. If you cannot access the app, you can submit a deletion request using the WhatsApp link below.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the MARU GHAR app and sign in to your customer account.
  2. In the Customer Account section, choose “Delete My Account.”
  3. Confirm the request. When it succeeds, your Firebase sign-in account and the customer data listed below are deleted.

What data is deleted

  • Customer sign-in account and private profile.
  • Saved/favorite property list.
  • Customer notifications and enquiries.
About your property listings

Public property listings you submitted, the owner's contact number, and Cloudinary photos/videos are not automatically removed by account deletion. Submit a separate request using the link below if you want these removed. The relevant listing and media will be removed after the request is verified.

Request deletion via WhatsApp

Include the email address or registered mobile number for your account so the request can be verified.
